Process Serving by JagX Security & Investigations


Reliable. Professional. Timely.


At JagX Security & Investigations, we provide professional process serving services across Michigan. Our experienced team ensures that legal documents are delivered promptly, accurately, and in compliance with all court requirements.

With backgrounds in federal law enforcement and military service, our process servers understand the importance of proper documentation and timely delivery. We serve a wide range of legal documents including summons, subpoenas, complaints, and other court papers.



Our Process Serving Approach

  1. Receive and review your court documents
  2. Locate the recipient and verify identity
  3. Serve documents in compliance with legal requirements
  4. Complete and file proof of service with the court

Why Choose JagX Security & Investigations?

  • Experienced professionals with law enforcement backgrounds
  • Reliable and timely document delivery
  • Full compliance with Michigan service requirements
  • Professional handling of all legal documents
