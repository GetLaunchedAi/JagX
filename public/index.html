About Us

About JagX Security & Investigations

JagX Security & Investigations is led by Mo Kazan, a seasoned federal law enforcement professional with over 20 years of experience in the Department of Homeland Security, Department of Defense, and the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

Our team is made up of veterans, former law enforcement officers, and highly trained security professionals. We specialize in personal protection, private estate security, consulting, surveillance, and intelligence services.

Our mission is simple: deliver reliable, professional, and effective security solutions. We pride ourselves on attention to detail, proven success, and consistent client relationships. Whether you need personal protection or large-scale security operations, JagX is the partner you can trust.
